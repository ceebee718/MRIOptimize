PROAQMR Protocol Optimizer

Includes 57 sequences from UW Madison MSK protocol sheets (GE SIGNA Artist 1.5 T and Architect 3 T). Enter an MRI protocol as it appears on the console, choose which parameters may change, and search for settings that cut scan time or raise SNR while staying inside SAR, resolution and chemical-shift limits.

Protocol

Coverage is held constant: if slice thickness changes, the slice or partition count is recalculated. Entering the console's own time and SAR calibrates the model to your scanner. Tissue relaxation values are approximate literature values; edit them for your population.

Locked rows stay at their current value. TE and flip angle are locked by default to keep image contrast.

Goal and limits

Normal operating mode whole-body SAR is 2.0 W/kg (IEC 60601-2-33); first level is 4.0 W/kg. Without a console SAR value, the limit is a percentage of the current protocol's SAR.

      Model

      SNR ∝ B0 · voxel volume · √(phase lines · partitions · averages ÷ bandwidth per pixel) ÷ (g√R) · tissue signal. Spin-echo signal uses (1 − e^(−TR/T1)) · e^(−TE/T2); spoiled GRE uses the Ernst equation with T2*. Scan time = TR × shots × averages × concatenations. SAR scales with B0² × Σ(flip angle²) per second. Echo spacing, minimum TE and slice timing are estimated from bandwidth and calibrated so the current protocol is valid. Predictions are estimates: confirm time and SAR on the console, verify SNR and uniformity on the ACR phantom, and have a radiologist review image contrast before clinical use.
