Protocol

Coverage is held constant: if slice thickness changes, the slice count is recalculated. Console time and SAR calibrate the model to your scanner. Relaxation times are approximate literature values.

Limits

Blurring is measured as echo-train duration ÷ tissue T2 (fast spin echo only). Normal-mode whole-body SAR is 2.0 W/kg (IEC 60601-2-33). Without a console SAR value, the SAR limit is a percentage of the current protocol. Editing any limit switches the strategy to Custom.

        Model

        SNR ∝ B0 · voxel volume · √(phase lines · partitions · averages ÷ bandwidth per pixel) ÷ (g√R) · tissue signal. Spin-echo signal uses (1 − e^(−TR/T1)) · e^(−TE/T2); spoiled GRE uses the Ernst equation with T2*. Scan time = TR × shots × averages × concatenations. SAR scales with B0² × Σ(flip angle²) per second. Blurring = echo-train duration ÷ T2. Echo spacing, minimum TE and slice timing are estimated from bandwidth and calibrated so the current protocol is valid. Deep-learning reconstruction is not modeled. Confirm time and SAR on the console, verify SNR and uniformity on the ACR phantom, and have a radiologist review contrast before clinical use.
